# Items: a near-miss name still finds the item, and a no-op call never costs the turn

[繁體中文](item_handling_friction_design_spec_zh.md) | [Docs index](../../README.md)

Category: `enhancement`. Status: **implemented**. Base: `main_v2` at `ac6c943` plus PR #241.

## Problem

A review of the item path against real play found that most of what a player feels as "the bot forgot my stuff" or "I had to say it three times" came from the tools refusing or silently doing nothing, and the turn validator then throwing the whole turn away:

- `remove_carried_item` matched the stored text exactly. "鑰匙" never removed "地下室鑰匙", the tool still answered `ok: true` with the pack unchanged, and `turn_resolution._mutation_evidence` treated an unchanged pack as a failed mutation (`inventory_or_combat_not_verified`). The player read "請再說一次你的行動", said it again, and hit the same wall.
- `add_carried_item` for an item already carried was a silent no-op with the same consequence: an `ok: true` that voided the turn.
- Any refused inventory call anywhere in the turn voided the turn, so the Keeper could not even retry with the right name.
- `add_carried_item` and `remove_carried_item` were not offered to the narrator that runs after a settled check, so an item found by a successful Spot Hidden could not be registered until the player sent another message.
- `/coc sheet` printed melee weapons under 彈藥 with no count, which reads as a firearm.
- The weapon catalog had Chinese aliases for a handful of weapons only; 小刀, 匕首, 菜刀, 開山刀, 球棒, 指虎 did not resolve, so a declared attack with any of them paused the fight in `NEEDS_RULING`. A weapon registered on the sheet under one of its names (`weapons["小刀"]`) did not count as owned because only `carried_items` was scanned.

## Change

- `inventory.match_carried_items(items, reference)`: exact, then case-insensitive, then the entries that contain the reference or (when two characters or longer) are contained by it; a one-character entry such as 信 is inside too many unrelated words (信號槍) to prove it is the thing named. `remove_carried_item` removes the stored entry it names (`removed` in the receipt), refuses with `refusal: item_not_held` and the pack listed when nothing matches, and with `refusal: ambiguous_item` and the candidates when several distinct entries match. The match and both refusals are decided inside the state transaction, against the pack as it is committed, not the caller's snapshot: a second 鑰匙 another commit added since is an ambiguity, and an entry another commit already took is `item_not_held`, never a silent no-op. Every receipt carries `changed` and `item`, the entry as the pack spells it ("Knife" for a "knife" the pack already held; the stored text a partial name removed), and `turn_delivery.observe_tool` says what the pack contains by that entry, compared the way the tool matched it, so the public line never contradicts the receipt.
- `add_carried_item` of an item already carried (case-insensitively) returns `ok: true, changed: false, already_carried: true` and writes nothing.
- `turn_resolution`: a no-op (`changed: false`) is neither evidence nor failure; a refused add or remove is ignored when another call of the same tool on the same investigator for the same item (either spelling contains the other) succeeded this turn: later (the Keeper retried) or earlier (the thing was already taken, and the retry found it gone). An unretried refusal after a real change, and a refused `transfer_item`, still void a completion claim, as before. A turn whose only inventory calls wrote nothing may end as `no_mechanics`. The legacy remove-then-add hand-off verifies against the entry a partial name removed.
- The Executor records no `inventory_change` event for a no-op.
- `add_carried_item` and `remove_carried_item` carry `resolved_check_followup`; the follow-up narrator's instruction names them.
- `Character.weapon_lines` prints 武器 for entries without ammunition and 彈藥 for the tracked ones, on both the sheet and the dynamic prompt line.
- `combat_weapons.json`: Chinese (and a few English) aliases for the melee and common firearm entries, each unique to one definition except the deliberately shared ones (手槍, 霰彈槍, and now 雙管霰彈槍, which names the 12, 16 and 20 gauge double barrels and so still needs the Keeper to pick the gauge). The weapons stay the CoC 7e table entries; a weapon the table marks as needing a ruling still returns `needs_ruling`.
- `combat_rules.resolve_weapon` resolves a reference that is not a catalog name by containment: the longest name or alias inside the reference, or that the reference is inside ("一把生鏽的小刀" is Knife, Small; "I have a large club" is Club, Large). Several definitions explained by names of the same length ("刀" is in every knife) stay `needs_ruling` with the candidates. Players write Traditional Chinese, which has no word boundaries, so containment rather than exact words is the match throughout; one-character aliases (矛, 弩) stay for the same reason.
- `combat_flow._weapon_actor_evidence` reads each carried item and each sheet `weapons` key the way a declaration is read (`_item_is_weapon`: exact name or alias, else the longest contained name, or the weapon among the candidates a short name such as 刀 lists; only an entry the catalog knows nothing by counts through the weapon's full name, for a scenario weapon the catalog does not list, so "thrusting sword" is the rapier and never the sword) and counts any weapon with no ammunition to track (melee or thrown) as owned when an entry is that weapon; 「手裏劍」 is the shuriken and never proves a sword. A firearm still needs its sheet entry, which the declaration may name by any of the weapon's spellings (`weapons["Crossbow"]` fired as 「十字弓」): the entry that is this weapon carries its ammunition.
- The catalog is reconciled with the 7e weapons table (`cthulhuwiki.chaosium.com/equipment/weapons.html`, a copy supplied by the operator): Spear 1D8+1 impaling with no damage bonus (Fighting (Spear)); Spear thrown 1D8+½DB impaling at STR/5 yards (Throw); Burning Torch 1D6 (Fighting (Brawl)); the table's burn (a Luck roll or the target catches fire) is the row's `follow_up`, with the burning damage fixed at 1D6 a round until put out as this table's house rule: the table's round-by-round escalation is deliberately simplified away, by the operator's decision, because the effect runner holds one amount per effect; a hit's result and the settled-check block hand it to the Keeper, who decides whether the target catches fire (an NPC has no Luck to roll) and registers the burning rounds with `declare_combat_effect` (offered to the follow-up narrator for this) at the fixed amount, since the engine does not roll it; Shuriken 1D3+½DB impaling at STR/5 yards, malfunction 100; Rock 1D4+½DB at STR/5 yards; Bullwhip 1D3+½DB (Fighting (Whip); its row becomes `melee`, it was a 3-yard `single_shot`) and Nunchaku 1D8+DB (Fighting (Flail)) resolve as ordinary melee attacks; Crossbow 1D8+2 impaling at 50 yards, one bolt, malfunction 96, and the single-round .45 Martini-Henry and Col. Moran's air rifle, resolve as single shots whose rate of fire the engine now keeps (`WeaponDefinition.rounds_per_shot`: 2 for the crossbow, 3 for the two rifles): a shot fired in round N holds the weapon until round N + rounds, and a declaration before that is refused with the round the next shot is possible in, not paused for a ruling, so the investigator does something else that round; a paused shot a ruling maps to a slow weapon is held by the same reload; Thompson 1D10+2 at 20 yards, Bergmann MP18 1D10 at 20 yards, the BAR M1918 and the Bren resolve as single shots (a burst or full auto is not an engine mode; the Browning M1917A1, Lewis and Vickers are full auto only and still need a ruling); a new Uzi 1D10 impaling at 20 yards, 32 rounds, malfunction 98 from the modern table. A thrown weapon's base range comes from the thrower (`WeaponDefinition.base_range_formula`, `combat_rules.base_range_for`), a sheet that spells the skill another way (「手槍」 for 射擊（手槍）, via the static alias table) is read as the investigator's own value, and an investigator whose sheet lacks the weapon's skill attacks at the 7e base value (Brawl 25, Spear 20, Sword 20, Axe 15, Whip 5, Flail 10, Garrote 15, Handgun 20, Rifle/Shotgun 25, Bow 15, SMG 15, MG 10, Throw 20) instead of being refused. The Garrote keeps its ruling: its hold is a manoeuvre, not a damage roll. Three Investigator Handbook melee rows join the catalog: Axe (wood or fire axe) 1D8+2+DB impaling (Fighting (Axe)), Sword (cavalry sabre, long sword) 1D8+DB impaling and Rapier 1D6+1+DB impaling (Fighting (Sword)); the price list's razor, horsewhip and billy club map to the small knife, the bullwhip and the small club.

`transfer_item` keeps its exact item match: [inventory transfer](../bug/inventory_transfer_design_spec.md) chose that deliberately for a hand-off between two investigators.

## Not done

- The rest of the transfer spec's open steps (removal quantity and reason, the server-side intent gate, items left in a room).
- Firearm references still need the exact sheet key for ammunition.
