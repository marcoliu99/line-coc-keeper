# Separate narrative presentation from world authority

The Keeper must preserve what players heard without treating every previous narration as a source of world truth. We will retain conversation history as presentation, while projecting consequential facts only from scenario evidence whose conditions have been met, committed events, verified fact/clue records, or an explicit authorized KP decision. Player claims and unverified records remain available for continuity and correction but cannot authorize later mechanics. This avoids a fixed per-turn fact-review model call and preserves the existing player correction path; a natural-language correction may directly repair nonconsequential presentation, while changes to resources, scenario facts, or mechanics require the corresponding authority and durable correction record.

## Considered options

- Treat all delivered Keeper prose as canon: preserves continuity but compounds a single mistaken quantity, item, or location into future rulings.
- Discard prior prose: prevents that amplification but loses what players actually saw and heard.
- Keep presentation and authority separately: retains both histories, at the cost of provenance and projection work.
